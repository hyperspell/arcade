# Granted

Hyperspell Arcade Nº5. A six-puzzle patchboard about the Content Rules work merged
September 8–11, 2026. Approximately five minutes, untimed, solo, no dependencies.

## Play

Choose a grant reason (Fetched, Rule, or Manual), then a document and an audience to
connect them. A cable represents one grant with that reason, not a bare statement of
visibility. Selecting an existing connection with the same reason removes it. An
accessible cable ledger provides a second way to remove connections. Press Audit to
check both missing and extra grants; errors explain the first incorrect connection.
The player can retry indefinitely, reset the board, or ask for the next correction.
Only first-audit successes count toward the optional end-of-run score.

The board changes between six dispatches: ownership; multiple owners; Inbox Share
and Dismiss; a live filter; archived sharing rules; a final combined audit. Three
audiences (two fictional people and the company) fit on both desktop and mobile.
All instructions and the ledger remain usable without animation or color perception.

## Semantics

- A personal fetch grants its owner. A document can have multiple owners.
- A sharing match is not a grant. This game's binary judge queues; an eligible owner
  must opt in where required and approve. Opt-in alone does not share anything.
- A live filter removes rule grants, including earlier shares, but preserves ingest
  and manual grants. No content is deleted by the game metaphor.
- An archived admin sharing rule does not revoke grants it already minted.
- Access is the union of grants. A company grant reaches both named people too.
  The exact provenance ledger is audited separately from the resulting readers.
- Inbox choices in the game describe decisions already made by fictional owners.
  The player implements those decisions, rather than impersonating the owner.

The final puzzle combines these semantics. Teardown re-apply, continuous ingestion
apply, scored auto-sharing, automatic opt-out revocation, production promotion, and
legacy cutover operations are not represented as completed work.

## Content and release boundary

The mechanics are grounded in the source contracts for storage, ingest, apply, and
personal opt-ins, checked against current main and merged PRs on September 11. The
game says this is a model of merged code, not a claim of a universal production
rollout. All people, documents, dialogue, scores, and puzzle sequences are fictional.
The public HTML contains no internal links, customer data, volumes, or rollout details.

## Acceptance

- Six puzzles can be completed using pointer, touch, or keyboard.
- Adding, removing, undoing, resetting, auditing, hints, next, and replay work.
- Audit checks exact typed grants, catches extras, and reports derived readers.
- Repeated routes cannot duplicate a grant; undo restores the previous board.
- Source selections and disabled progression cannot carry across dispatches.
- All game assets are inline; local storage is best-score-only and failure-tolerant.
- The arcade index and README include the new game.
